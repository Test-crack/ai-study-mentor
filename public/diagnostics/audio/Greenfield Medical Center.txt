Welcome to Greenfield Medical
Centre. This recorded message
contains important information
for all patients. Greenfield
Medical Centre is open Monday to
Friday from 8 in the morning
until 6 in the evening. On
Saturdays we are open from 9
until 1 o'. Clock. We are closed
on Sundays and on public
holidays. If you need urgent
medical help outside of these
hours, please call the NHS
Urgent Care line. To book an
appointment with a doctor, you
can call our reception on
012-456-7890 or you can use our
online booking system at
www.greenfieldmedical.co.uk.
most appointments last 10
minutes. If you think you will
need more time, please let our
receptionist know when you book
and we will arrange a longer
appointment for you. When you
arrive at the centre, please
check in at the main reception
desk on the ground floor. You
will then be asked to take a
seat in the waiting area. If you
cannot attend your appointment,
please cancel at least two hours
before your scheduled time. This
allows us to offer your
appointment to another patient.
We also offer a nurses clinic
every Tuesday and Thursday
morning. The nurse can help with
blood tests, vaccinations,
dressing wounds and health
checks. No appointment is
necessary for the nurses clinic.
For patients who need repeat
prescriptions, these can be
requested online or by speaking
to the receptionist. Please
allow two working days for your
prescription to be ready.
Greenfield Medical Centre also
offers a mental health support
service. If you are feeling
stressed, anxious or low in
mood, please speak to your
doctor who can refer you to one
of our counsellors. There is
free parking available at the
rear of the building with spaces
reserved for patients with
disabilities near the main
entrance. The centre is
accessible by bus. Routes number
7 and 15 stop directly in front
of the building. For all non
urgent enquiries, you can email
us@inforeenfieldmedical.co.uk we
aim to respond within one
working day. Thank you for
choosing Greenfield Medical
Centre. We look forward to
seeing you.
