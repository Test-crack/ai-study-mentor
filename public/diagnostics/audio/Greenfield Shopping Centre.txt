Welcome to Greenfield Shopping
center, the newest and largest
shopping destination in the
city. We are delighted to be
open seven days a week from 9 in
the morning until 9 in the
evening on weekdays and from 10
until 6 on Sundays. Greenfield
Shopping center has over 120
shops on three floors offering
everything from fashion and
electronics to food and
homeware. On the ground floor
you will find our main entrance,
the customer information desk
and our large supermarket, Fresh
Mart. Freshmart is open from 8
in the morning, one hour before
the rest of the center opens so
you can do your grocery shopping
early. On the first floor you
will find clothing stores, a
bookshop, a pharmacy and a
children's play area. The
children's play area is free to
use and is supervised by trained
staff between 10 in the morning
and 5 in the afternoon. On the
second floor you will find our
food court with 12 different
restaurants and cafes serving
food from around the world.
There is also a cinema with six
screens and a large sports shop
called Active Zone. We have
parking for over 800 cars in our
multi story car park. The first
two hours are free and after
that it costs £2 per hour. We
also have bicycle racks at the
main entrance if you prefer to
cycle here. For those traveling
by public Transport, buses
number 4, 9 and 22 all stop
directly outside the center. If
you need any assistance during
your visit, please visit the
customer information desk on the
ground floor or ask any member
of our staff. We hope you enjoy
your visit to Greenfield
Shopping Center. Thank you.
